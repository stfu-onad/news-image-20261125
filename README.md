A one-page newspaper front page summing up Wednesday 25 November
2026's world news in a single image.

Four main stories: Zelensky confirming Ukraine sank two Russian
ships — a cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland and a warship off
Benghazi, Libya — with Moscow calling the strikes "terrorism";
COP31 collapsing in Antalya with no deal as oil and gas producers
block fossil-fuel phaseout language; Houthi strikes hitting
Riyadh's tallest tower, wounding 11, as rebels push toward Aden;
and Trump awarding Rudy Giuliani the Medal of Freedom, plus
pardoning Thanksgiving turkeys Freedom and Feathers. Ticker:
Sarkozy ordered to trial, NATO jets for Poland, Iran tankers hit
in Hormuz, Gaza toll 74,746, 700,000 Russian troops killed per a
new analysis, and England beating Australia in the 2nd T20.

The hero illustration was generated in the builder session (no text
in it — a burning Russian cargo ship at dusk, a drone overhead, a
warship sinking in the distance); all headlines, the date
"WEDNESDAY, 25 NOVEMBER 2026" and captions were drawn programmatically
(Python + PIL, DejaVu fonts) so every word is crisp and legible.
`main.py` copies the shipped image to `/output/entry.png` after
checking it is a valid PNG within the size and dimension limits.
